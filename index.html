how to play

Hold both hands up to the mirror. The left hand picks the chord. The right hand decides how it sounds.

left hand · the chord

  • tilt inmajor
  • tilt outminor
  • 1 to 5 fingersdegrees I–V
  • index + pinkyVI
  • those + thumbVII

right hand · the color

  • 1 fingerroot
  • 2 fingers1st inversion
  • 3 fingers7th
  • 4 fingersdominant or dim
  • thumb outlower octave
  • tilt indarker filter
  • tilt outbrighter filter
  • hand uplouder
  • hand downsofter
